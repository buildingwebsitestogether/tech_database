About Me

Hey! I'm Sam, a high school student currently learning to build web projects in Mr. Smith's Building With AI class. As a dedicated high school basketball player who loves the game, I wanted to create something inspired by my passion on the court.

That drive led me to design and code Basketball Chaos Arcade using canvas physics and synthetic sound effects! When I'm off the court and outside of class, I enjoy reading, exploring new web technologies, and expanding this portfolio.

Basketball Chaos Highlights

  • Realistic trajectory aiming arc with adjustable power vectors
  • Pure Web Audio API SFX (Swish, Bounce, Rim Clang, Coin pickup)
  • Full browser Back-button navigation support & offline download
  • In-game Chaos Shop with Fireball skin & extended guides
