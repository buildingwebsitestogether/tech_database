IDENTITY. Privacy Policy

Please read this policy before using Identity Playbook. It explains what is stored, who can see it, and how to download or delete it.

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. About Identity Playbook

Identity Playbook is a basketball playbook community. You can draw and save plays, attach images or PDFs, and choose to share plays with other signed-in users.

2. What we store about you

  • Account: your email address and a random account ID. Sign-in is handled by Supabase Authentication (and GitHub if you choose it). Your password is never stored by this app's own tables.
  • Profile: your chosen username and your light/dark theme preference.
  • Plays: title, type, description, court diagram, visibility (public or private) and timestamps.
  • Files: images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF) and PDFs up to 10 MB each, with an optional description for accessibility.
  • Reports and appeals: anything you write when you report a play or appeal a restriction.
  • Moderation records: if staff act on your content or account (for example hiding a play or suspending an account), the action, reason, date and the staff member responsible are recorded.

3. Who can see what

  • Only you can see your private plays, your private files, your reports and your appeals.
  • Other signed-in users can see plays you mark Public (with your username and any attached files). Signed-out visitors cannot see plays.
  • Moderators can read any play or file when reviewing a report, hide or restore plays, review reports, and suspend accounts temporarily. They can request a permanent block.
  • Administrators can do everything moderators can, plus permanently remove plays and files, block or unblock accounts, review appeals, manage moderators, and read the moderation history.
  • These permissions are enforced by the database itself (Row Level Security and secured functions), not only by the buttons you see on screen.
  • Staff accounts show a MOD or ADMIN badge. Usernames that imitate staff are reserved.

4. Reporting and moderation

Signed-in users can report a public play and must explain the problem. Reports are visible to you and to staff. Hidden plays and their files are no longer visible to other users but remain visible to you and to staff until an administrator permanently removes them. Moderation records are kept so that decisions can be reviewed and appealed.

5. How your information is used

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To display your username, plays and files as you have chosen.
  • To keep the community safe through reports, moderation and appeals.

6. Services that process information

Identity Playbook uses Supabase for authentication, database and file storage, and GitHub when you choose GitHub sign-in. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These services have their own privacy policies. This app does not request your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

7. Data stored in your browser

Your browser stores a few small settings: that you accepted this policy, your theme choice, which announcement you dismissed, and your sign-in session. Your theme is also saved to your profile so it follows you across devices.

8. Download your data

In Account → Export my data you can download a JSON file with your account, profile, plays, file details, reports you sent, restrictions and appeals.

9. Deleting your content and your data

  • Delete individual items: you can delete any play or file at any time. You will be asked to confirm first.
  • Delete My Data: in Account you can delete your whole account. This immediately removes your sign-in account, profile, username, plays, attachments, uploaded files and appeals.
  • What remains: reports you filed are kept without your identity so moderators can finish reviewing them. Moderation records and restrictions are kept using only your random account ID (no email or username) so that bans and past decisions stay accurate.
  • Backups: our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a limited time before they expire.

10. Security

Files are stored in a private bucket with access rules, and each account can only change its own content. No online service can promise absolute security, so please do not upload sensitive or confidential documents.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way you will be asked to read and accept it again.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact the person or organization responsible for operating Identity Playbook.
