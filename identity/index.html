IDENTITY. Privacy Policy

Please read this policy before using Identity Playbook. It explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. About Identity Playbook

Identity Playbook is a basketball playbook application. It allows users to create, save, organize, and manage basketball plays and upload related files.

2. Information associated with your account

When you create an account or sign in, the app uses account information such as your email address, account identifier, and chosen username.

You can sign in using email and password or GitHub. Authentication is handled through Supabase and, when applicable, GitHub's sign-in service. Your password is handled by Supabase Authentication rather than being stored in the playbook tables by this app.

3. Your basketball plays

When you create or edit a play, the app saves information such as its name, type, description, court diagram, and related timestamps.

This information is stored in the app's Supabase database and is associated with your account.

4. Uploaded files

The app allows you to upload PDF files and images in JPG, JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP format. Each file must be no larger than 10 MB.

Uploaded files are stored in the Supabase Storage bucket named identity-playbook-files, under a folder associated with your account.

Access to files depends on the Supabase Storage security policies configured for this project. Do not upload sensitive or confidential documents.

5. How your information is used

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Display your username and personal playbook.
  • Save and retrieve your basketball plays.
  • Upload, download, and delete your files.
  • Maintain your sign-in session.

6. Services that process information

Identity Playbook uses Supabase for authentication, database storage, and file storage. GitHub may process information when you choose GitHub sign-in.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of information is also subject to their own settings and practices.

7. Information this app does not directly request

The current app code does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location. This does not mean that the hosting or authentication services collect no technical information.

8. Account security and privacy

Your account is intended to access its own saved plays and files. Supabase Row Level Security and Storage policies are important parts of protecting that data.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in details private and avoid uploading information you would not want stored online.

9. Your choices and deleting information

You can sign out, edit or delete saved plays, and delete uploaded files using the available app features. Deleting a play or file does not automatically delete your entire account.

The current app does not provide a complete account deletion feature. Account deletion and any associated data removal may require assistance from the app administrator.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. If an updated policy requires acceptance, you may be asked to read and accept it again.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact the person or organization responsible for operating Identity Playbook.
